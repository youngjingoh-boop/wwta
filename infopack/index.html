Who we are

Worldwide Tennis Academy has coached across Sydney since 2010. Every session is built on scientifically proven technique and tactics — and on the simple belief that tennis should be enjoyed. Today that's more than 2,300 members, a ten-strong team and courts from Granville to Killara, where first-timers, families, social players and future pros share the game.

Our mission

To cultivate a culture of tennis for young professional athletes, provide social tennis for everyone, and put the enjoyment of the game first.
