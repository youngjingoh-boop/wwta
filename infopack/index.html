01 — Who we are

More than a court.
A community.

Worldwide Tennis Academy has coached Sydney since 2010. What began on a single court is now a 300-plus member community across four locations — beginners picking up a racquet for the first time, juniors on a pathway to US college scholarships, and adults who come to switch off and play.

Tennis is the reason people arrive. The coaching, the friendships and the feeling of getting better are why they stay. Whatever your age or level, there's a place for you on our courts.
